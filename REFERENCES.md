# Knowledge References

Sources the dictionary *learns from* but does not ship. Where
`LIBRARIES.md` registers code you install, this file registers bodies of
knowledge you consult and cite. Each row records what the source covers,
how it may be used, and which entries it informs. Licence terms decide
the usage column — check them before drawing more from any source.

## Interface Lineage — interfacelineage.com (registered 2026-11)

**What it is.** A library of named interaction patterns and principles
(Johannes Mutter): each entry frames a user problem, the mechanism that
solves it, when not to use it, related patterns, and its history. About
80 patterns across collaboration, command surfaces, direct manipulation,
feeds, navigation, notification, reversibility, spatial input, system
feedback, and touch, plus interface case studies and principles.

**Usage: cite and link only.** Its terms allow quoting, citing, linking,
and reading pages to answer a question. They reserve keeping a
substantial copy, building one "by repeatedly copying small parts," and
using the pages as training data. So:

- Never copy or paraphrase its entries section by section, and never
  mirror its pattern list as our entry list.
- Our `behavior` entries are written in our own words from general
  practice, clustered to our own granularity, and link Interface Lineage
  pages under Starting points as further reading.
- Its history and lineage material is out of scope here by design: the
  dictionary records what a behavior is, what it does, and when to use it.
- A paid membership adds an MCP token for live queries; its terms forbid
  bulk-downloading the corpus through that token.

**Informs:** the `behavior` category — [[undo-recovery]],
[[drag-and-drop]], [[snap-and-guides]], [[selection-model]],
[[continuous-controls]], [[pointer-intent]], [[touch-gestures]],
[[inline-commands]], [[autocomplete-typeahead]], [[live-feedback]],
[[status-signals]], [[interaction-principles]],
[[presence-collaboration]], [[feed-pagination]],
[[overview-navigation]], [[panes-and-tiling]], [[ai-interaction]],
[[block-editor]], [[spatial-input]].

**When to consult it.** A behavior request with no entry, or a designer
asking why a pattern exists: read the relevant page, answer, cite the URL.

## design.how — design.how (registered 2026-11)

**What it is.** Arlan Marat's design systems, skills, and visuals for
coding agents (github.com/arla6ka/design.how, MIT).

**Usage: MIT — adapt with credit.** Two parts are used here:

- **The four systems, as wardrobes.** Their aesthetic and behavior rules
  (not their component code) are written up as wardrobe entries: [[calm-console]] (Quartz),
  [[mono-infra]] (Sodium), [[warm-consumer]] (Lime), [[pressable-glass]] (Glaze).
- **The Vault, as a library.** 72 effect skills, registered and
  categorized in `LIBRARIES.md` under "design.how Vault", with
  Starting-points lines on the entries they implement.

Its review and migration skills are out of scope.
