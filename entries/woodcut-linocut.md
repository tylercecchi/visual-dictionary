# Woodcut / Linocut

Carved-block printmaking as a look — bold figures cut from black, white
lines gouged out by hand, rough edges where the blade fought the grain.
[[hatching-sketch]]'s heavier sibling: hatching *draws* dark lines on
light; woodcut *carves* light lines out of dark.

## You know it when you see

- High-contrast imagery that reads as black shapes with white cuts inside — the tonal logic inverted from drawing
- Line endings that taper to blade-points; slightly ragged, hand-cut edges; no perfect curves
- Chunky, expressive figures (the German Expressionist register) or folk/gig-poster boldness
- Ink texture: solid blacks with tiny white speckle where the block didn't press

## Dials

- **carve logic (the defining dial)** — the mark is *removal*: whites are cuts in a black field, so shading works by cutting more (light areas = dense white cuts, shadows = untouched black); art directing means thinking in what to remove — the exact inverse of [[hatching-sketch]]
- **blade character** — cuts taper at both ends (the gouge's entry and exit), curve stiffly (blades resist), and wobble slightly; parallel-cut shading fans follow form crudely; too-smooth curves are the instant tell of fake woodcut
- **edge raggedness** — shape boundaries carry small nicks and chips ([[dissolve-disintegration]] erosion at whisper level); linocut is smoother/rounder than woodcut (no grain fighting back) — the two registers differ by exactly this dial
- **ink honesty** — solid blacks with press texture (sparse white speckle, heavier at large fields — [[film-grain]] inverted), slight ink squash at edges; one ink is the core register; two-block color (black + one flat spot color, deliberately misregistered — [[print-artifacts]]) is the classic extension
- **register** — Expressionist (crude, emotional, faces in few violent cuts), folk/devotional (symmetric, decorative — [[ornament-traditions]] adjacency), modern gig-poster (clean linocut + bold [[type-composition]]), ex-libris (fine, dense)

## Techniques

- **SVG carving** — draw the *white cuts* as tapered filled paths
  (both-ends-pointed lens shapes) over black shape fields; roughen
  contours with subtle turbulence displacement; the drawing workflow
  literally mirrors carving.
- **Image conversion** — threshold a photo hard, then replace gray
  ramps with directional cut-fans (a [[hatching-sketch]]-style stroke
  fill whose strokes are white-on-black and tapered); pure
  auto-threshold without cut logic reads as Xerox, not woodcut.
- **Texture pass** — press speckle via [[film-grain]] masked to black
  fields; paper ground ([[material-texture]]) under everything;
  misregistered spot color per [[print-artifacts]].
- **Type** — carved lettering as white-cut letterforms in a black
  cartouche, or heavy slab/blackletter faces roughened to match.
