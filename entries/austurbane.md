# Austurbane

A late-2010s-to-now brand register named in the CARI aesthetics
catalogue: an urbane, restrained offshoot of Corporate Memphis. The flat
cartoons and loud pastels go; serif type, muted neutrals and metallic
accents arrive, with lots of whitespace and calm, natural materials. It's
the look of premium direct-to-consumer brands, skincare, hospitality and
architecture studios.

## You know it when you see

- Serif headlines (often light or medium weight) over small, well-spaced
  sans body text
- Black, beige, stone, warm grey, with copper, bronze or gold as the only
  accents
- Big margins and empty space; one product or image per view
- Product photography from above or isometric, staged with sharper,
  more dramatic light than Corporate Memphis's flat cheerfulness
- Natural "zen" materials: stone, linen, ceramics, wood, plants
- The failure: beige-on-beige with no hierarchy, where everything is
  tasteful and nothing is findable

## Dials

- **type voice**: a refined serif for display (light/regular,
  sentence case), quiet grotesk for UI and body ([[typographic-voice]]);
  small caps or tracked labels sparingly
- **palette**: neutral ground (bone, beige, stone, near-black), one
  metallic accent used for small things (rules, icons, a price, the CTA
  outline); [[color-roles]] accent budget very tight
- **metal rendering**: flat metallic colour (copper `#b87333`-ish) vs
  a subtle [[specular-metallic]] sheen on foil-like details; flat is the
  default on screen
- **space**: generous, airy [[spacing-density]]; one idea per viewport
- **imagery**: staged still life with directional light, natural
  materials, warm tone ([[image-treatment]])
- **motion**: slow, quiet fades and gentle parallax; no bounce
  ([[motion-ceremony]] low)
- **shape**: small radii or none; thin hairline borders ([[border-stroke]])

## Techniques

- **Type pairing**: a serif with good light weights (e.g. a Didone or
  humanist serif) for headings, a neutral sans at small size; large size
  contrast between them carries the hierarchy so colour doesn't have to.
- **Metal as a token**: define one `--accent-metal` and use it for rules,
  icons, focus rings and one CTA; check contrast (copper on beige often
  fails for text; use it decoratively, keep text dark).
- **Photography**: overhead/isometric product shots, shadows kept crisp,
  backgrounds in the palette's neutrals.
- **Layout**: editorial grid with wide margins ([[grid-systems]]); a
  [[magazine-spread]] or [[product-detail]] structure fits.
