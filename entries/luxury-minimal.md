# Luxury Minimal (Fashion-House Minimalism)

The minimalism of fashion houses and high-end galleries: vast empty
space, very small type, black and white (or one neutral), a single
full-bleed image at a time, a wordmark, and almost no interface chrome.
The restraint itself signals status. It's colder and more severe than
[[austurbane]]'s warm, metallic DTC luxury, and sparser than
[[fashion-editorial]]'s magazine layouts.

## You know it when you see

- A full-screen photograph, a small wordmark, and almost nothing else
- Navigation in tiny uppercase or small sans, at the edges
- Black, white, maybe one stone or off-white; no accent colour at all
- Product grids with huge gutters, prices in small plain type
- Slow fades between images; no bounce, no badges, no banners
- The failure: so sparse it hides function. Search, size selection, cart
  and checkout still need to be findable and accessible

## Dials

- **emptiness**: how much space surrounds each element; the defining
  dial ([[spacing-density]] at its airiest)
- **type size**: small and quiet (11-14px UI, modest headings) vs one
  very large wordmark; avoid mid-size everything
- **type family**: neutral grotesk (severe, modern) or refined serif
  (classic house); one family, little weight variation
  ([[typographic-voice]])
- **palette**: pure monochrome vs one neutral ground; accent budget zero
  ([[color-roles]]), so state and errors need shape and words, not colour
- **imagery**: full-bleed, high-production photography carries all the
  emotion ([[image-treatment]]); one image per view
- **motion**: slow crossfades (400-800ms), no spring, no overshoot
  ([[motion-ceremony]] minimal, ceremonial pace)
- **chrome**: hidden or hairline; menus open as full-screen overlays

## Techniques

- **Layout**: full-viewport image sections, a fixed tiny header, product
  grids with large gaps; `aspect-ratio` to keep images consistent.
- **Type tokens**: a very small UI size and a restrained scale, letter-spaced
  uppercase for nav only, sentence case elsewhere.
- **Image performance**: responsive `srcset`, AVIF/WebP, and lazy
  loading. A luxury site that loads slowly fails its own premise.
- **Usability floor**: visible focus states, readable price/size
  information, a findable cart; minimal is not missing.

## Starting points

- Reference sites: fashion houses and luxury retailers (The Row, Jil
