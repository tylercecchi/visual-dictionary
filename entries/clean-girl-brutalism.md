# Clean Girl Brutalism (Soft / Neutral Brutalism)

A hybrid register, more trend phrase than established movement:
brutalism's structure (exposed grid, hard rules, oversized type, no
decoration) dressed in the "clean girl" palette from beauty and fashion
(cream, beige, nude, soft glow, glossy restraint). It's raw in layout and
polished in surface. It suits beauty, wellness, fashion and lifestyle
brands that want edge without noise.

## You know it when you see

- Huge, tight grotesk or mono headlines set on a visible grid, but in
  espresso or warm black on cream instead of black on white
- Hairline rules, boxed sections and exposed structure, with no thick
  neo-brutalist borders or hard offset shadows
- A palette of cream, oat, sand, latte, nude pink, one deep brown
- Glossy, dewy product photography cropped hard into grid cells
- The failure: losing either half. Too soft and it's just beige minimal
  ([[austurbane]]); too loud and it's [[brutalism]] with a filter

## Dials

- **rawness**: how much structure shows. Visible grid lines, index
  numbers, raw labels (high) vs implied grid only (low). Keep it high or
  the register disappears
- **type scale violence**: display type very large and tight (true to
  brutalism) against small, plain body text; one family or a grotesk +
  mono pair ([[typographic-voice]])
- **palette warmth**: cream to latte ground, warm near-black text, one
  muted accent (nude, terracotta, olive); no saturated flats
- **border language**: 1px hairlines, square corners; no hard shadows,
  no blur ([[border-stroke]], [[shape-language]] radius 0)
- **surface gloss**: matte UI, glossy imagery. Shine lives in the
  photos (dewy skin, glazed product), never in the chrome
- **density**: brutalist grids can run dense; the palette needs air.
  Moderate [[spacing-density]] with generous outer margins

## Techniques

- **Grid as decoration**: CSS grid with visible 1px rules (borders on
  cells or a `background-image` grid), numbered sections, raw metadata
  labels in mono.
- **Type**: `font-size: clamp()` for very large headings with tight
  `line-height` (0.9-1) and negative tracking; body at a modest size.
- **Palette tokens**: cream ground, warm-black text, one muted accent;
  check contrast for small mono labels on cream.
- **Imagery**: hard-cropped glossy photography filling grid cells, no
  rounded corners or drop shadows.
