# Retro-Futurism

The imagined-futures wardrobe — each past era's vision of tomorrow, worn
as a look. Naming the era loads a complete costume of materials, type,
and machinery from existing entries; this entry is the axis and its
recipes.

## You know it when you see

- **Steampunk** (Victorian future): brass, gears, rivets, leather, gauges
- **Dieselpunk** (30s-40s): riveted steel, streamline curves, propaganda-poster type
- **Atompunk/rocketpunk** (50s space age): rockets, atoms, boomerang curves, starbursts, googie signage
- **Cassette futurism** (70s-80s): beige plastic, CRT green, chunky buttons, wood-grain tech
- **Cybercore** (90s-00s cyber): circuit traces, neon-on-black, chrome wireframes

## Dials

- **era pick (the master dial)** — each era fixes materials, palette, and type together; mixing eras reads as costume-shop unless deliberate
- **steampunk recipe** — [[specular-metallic]] brass/copper + [[material-texture]] leather + [[weathering-patina]] (tarnish, not rust) + [[skeuomorphism]] gauges and hardware controls + Victorian display type ([[historical-ornate]] type wing) + [[blueprint]]'s engraved-diagram register
- **atompunk recipe** — boomerang/kidney shapes and starbursts ([[memphis-style]]'s ancestors), [[art-deco]]-descended googie signage with [[neon-signage]], atomic orbit motifs ([[radial-orbital]] geometry as ornament), optimistic pastels + chrome ([[color-roles]]: turquoise-coral-cream)
- **dieselpunk recipe** — riveted [[specular-metallic]] steel, streamline curves, [[duotone-gradient-map]] propaganda grading, condensed poster type ([[type-composition]]), [[print-artifacts]] wear
- **cassette-futurism recipe** — beige/orange plastic ([[rubber-soft-touch]] molded forms), [[crt-scanlines]] phosphor displays, chunky [[skeuomorphism]] buttons, [[terminal-aesthetic]] readouts; the register of *tactile* tech
- **cybercore recipe** — [[pcb-circuit]] traces + [[fui]] linework + [[neon-signage]] on black + [[glitch]] + [[chromatic-aberration]]; overlaps [[period-looks]]' Y2K but harder-tech
- **sincerity dial** — played straight (world-building, games, brand lore) vs winked (one brass gauge in a modern UI); full costumes are showcase territory, single borrowed elements are the product-safe register

## Techniques

Wardrobe entries compose; the technique is the recipe list above plus:
- **Machinery as ornament** — gears/rockets/orbits as SVG symbol kits
  with [[generative-art]] constructed geometry (gear teeth are math).
- **Era-correct wear** — [[weathering-patina]] tuned per era (brass
  tarnish vs steel grime vs plastic yellowing).
- **Type sourcing** — each era has revival faces (search per era name);
  verify licensing.
