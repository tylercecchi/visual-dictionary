# Art Deco

The machine-age luxury wardrobe — 1920s-30s geometry worn as glamour:
sunbursts, stepped forms, gold on black, symmetric frames. "Gatsby
style" loads this whole costume; its power is that ornament is built
from *ruler-and-compass geometry*, so it reproduces crisply in vector.

## You know it when you see

- Radiating sunburst/fan motifs; chevrons and zigzags in disciplined rows
- Stepped, ziggurat-like forms (the skyscraper silhouette); strict bilateral symmetry
- Gold/brass linework on deep black, emerald, or burgundy grounds
- Tall, geometric display type with high waists and inline strokes

## Dials

- **motif vocabulary** — sunburst (the signature: rays from a point or arc), fan/shell, chevron rows, stepped frames, stylized fountains and wings; motifs are *constructed* (equal angular divisions, arithmetic steps — [[generative-art]] discipline), never freehand
- **symmetry law** — compositions center on a vertical axis; frames and cartouches enclose content ([[visual-composition]] formal-balance register at its most extreme); asymmetric deco reads as pastiche error
- **palette** — metallic + dark luxury: gold/champagne on black is the core ([[specular-metallic]] gradient-gold for the lines, or flat brass for the print register); jade, burgundy, cream as seconds; the [[color-roles]] triad is black-gold-one-jewel
- **type register** — geometric sans with high or low waists, generous tracking, all-caps display; inline/bifurcated strokes for the full costume ([[typographic-voice]]: Poiret-One/Marcellus territory; verify faces at use time)
- **line discipline** — consistent thin strokes ([[border-stroke]] hairline-to-light), double-rule frames, corner ornaments; metallic gradients on strokes ([[gradients]] vertical gold ramp) separate premium from flat
- **depth register** — flat poster (print deco) vs dimensional (beveled gold, [[light-on-flat]] sheen, marble grounds [[stone-veining]] — the hotel-lobby register)

## Techniques

- **SVG construction** — sunbursts as rotated line/wedge arrays, steps
  as arithmetic rect stacks, frames as stroked paths with corner motifs;
  the geometry is loops, not drawing ([[generative-art]] kinship).
- **CSS** — chevron/zigzag via `repeating-linear-gradient`; gold text
  via [[gradients]] `background-clip: text` ramps (champagne →
  gold → shadow); double borders + offset outlines for frames.
- **The full costume** — deco chrome ([[specular-metallic]]), marble or
  velvet grounds ([[stone-veining]], [[velvet-plush]]), warm key light
  ([[light-on-flat]]); restraint: ornament frames content, never floods
  it.
