# Surrealism

The dream-logic wardrobe — imagery that renders the impossible with
straight-faced realism: doors opening onto skies, floating stones,
melting objects, rooms that are also oceans. Its power move is
*technical sincerity*: the more realistically the impossible is
rendered, the harder it lands.

## You know it when you see

- Ordinary objects in impossible relationships — scale swapped, gravity ignored, interiors containing weather
- The Magritte register: crisp, calm, daylight rendering of one quiet impossibility
- The Dalí register: melting, stretching, hybrid forms in vast dream-lit landscapes
- Collage surrealism: photographic elements recombined at wrong scales ([[collage-scatter]] gone oneiric)

## Dials

- **the one-violation law** — strong surrealism breaks exactly one rule per image (scale OR gravity OR material OR context) and renders everything else obediently; multiple simultaneous violations read as fantasy-generic, not surreal ([[visual-composition]] discipline making the strange legible)
- **violation vocabulary** — scale swap (the moon as a lamp, a chair-sized apple), gravity release (floating stones, rising rain), material transfer (a stone cloud, melting solid — [[liquid-ripple]]/[[dissolve-disintegration]] machinery on the wrong objects), context graft (a door in the sea, sky indoors), hybrid splice (fish-bicycle)
- **rendering sincerity** — the register's engine: consistent light ([[light-on-flat]]/[[scene-staging]] one-sun law *especially* for impossible elements — the floating rock casts a correct [[ambient-occlusion]] ground shadow), correct [[depth-of-field]], photographic or classical-painting finish; sketchy rendering forfeits the uncanny
- **mood register** — Magritte-calm (daylight, bowler-hat deadpan, [[time-of-day]] noon), Dalí-vast (long shadows, dream horizons, [[time-of-day]] golden-empty), dark-uncanny ([[historical-ornate]] tenebrism + fog), digital-clean (the 3D render register: [[scene-staging]] pastel limbo + one impossible object — the current brand-surrealism default)
- **UI deployment** — hero images and empty states carrying one quiet impossibility (the floating-elements hero is domesticated surrealism); [[forced-perspective]] interactions (scroll revealing the trick); 404s as the natural habitat (the page that doesn't exist, rendered as a place that can't)

## Techniques

- **Compositing** — photographic cutouts recombined with unified
  grading ([[image-treatment]]: one grade over all sources — the
  mismatched-grade collage is the amateur tell), painted-in contact
  shadows ([[ambient-occlusion]]) grounding the impossible.
- **3D register** — [[scene-staging]] limbo + physically-lit impossible
  arrangements (floating via hidden supports removed in render);
  drei/three.js scenes where the physics simply differ.
- **Living surrealism** — subtle [[motion-ceremony]]-ambient loops:
  the floating object bobbing, the melt slowly progressing
  ([[morphing]] at dream tempo), clouds drifting through the interior.
- **The scroll reveal** — [[scroll-choreography]] staging the
  violation's discovery (the room assembles into an ocean as you
  scroll — [[forced-perspective]]'s privileged-viewpoint machinery).
