# Mid-Century Modern

The optimistic-modern wardrobe — 1945-65 American/Scandinavian design
language: Saul Bass paper-cut graphics, organic kidney-and-boomerang
shapes, atomic starbursts, mustard-teal-orange warmth. Modernism's
friendly decade: [[bauhaus-swiss]] discipline thawed by humor and
domestic optimism. "Mad Men era," constantly requested.

## You know it when you see

- Flat cut-paper shapes with deliberately imperfect edges — figures and objects as torn/cut silhouettes (the Bass title-sequence register)
- Organic asymmetric blobs (kidney, boomerang, palette shapes) and thin-line starbursts/atoms as ornament
- The palette: mustard, burnt orange, teal, olive, warm cream — saturated but *earthed*
- Textured flat color (print grain), jaunty diagonal compositions, spindly line-legs under solid shapes

## Dials

- **cut-paper register (Bass)** — figures reduced to flat silhouettes with slightly irregular edges (cut, not drawn — a controlled wobble distinct from [[cute-naive]]'s: confident scissor-cuts, not shaky lines), one shape color each, composed with [[visual-composition]] diagonal energy; type hand-cut-looking to match
- **shape vocabulary** — organic asymmetric blobs (kidney/boomerang/amoeba — drawn with 2-3 curvature changes, never symmetric — [[shape-language]] organic register with period grammar), thin-line starbursts and atom orbits ([[retro-futurism]]'s atompunk shares these; MCM uses them *quietly*, as wallpaper not spectacle), spindly legs/struts under solid masses (the furniture silhouette)
- **the earthed palette** — saturation with brown in it: mustard not yellow, burnt orange not orange, teal not cyan, olive, warm cream grounds ([[color-roles]]: the whole palette warm-shifted; pure primaries would tip it to [[bauhaus-swiss]])
- **texture truth** — flat color carries print grain ([[film-grain]]/[[print-artifacts]] silkscreen register), slight misregistration between shape and its outline; clean vector MCM reads as clip-art — the period *was* printed
- **composition energy** — jaunty diagonals and scatter with underlying calm ([[collage-scatter]] discipline), generous cream air; playful but never chaotic — the temperament between Bauhaus's cool and Memphis's loud (its direct descendant)
- **type voice** — mid-century grotesks and warm scripts; hand-lettered-adjacent display ([[typographic-voice]]: Futura-era geometrics softened); all-lowercase friendliness

## Techniques

- **Cut shapes** — SVG paths with subtle node irregularity (slight
  random perturbation, seeded — [[generative-art]] seed law) or
  authored in-tool; one flat fill each + optional offset outline
  (misregistered per [[print-artifacts]]).
- **Pattern kits** — starbursts (rotated line arrays), atomic orbits
  (ellipse trios), boomerang tilings as `<pattern>`s at low contrast
  for grounds.
- **The print pass** — grain overlay + warm cream ground +
  misregistration; the three moves that separate period-real from
  clip-art.
- **Deployment** — illustration systems ([[illustration-style]] register
  choice), brand moments, title-sequence-style [[kinetic-type]] intros
  (Bass animated: shapes sliding in with [[motion-ceremony]] jazz
  timing); pairs naturally with [[book-design]]/[[fashion-editorial]]
  structures per the structure × wardrobe doctrine.
