# Bauhaus / Swiss Modernism

The modernist wardrobe — the 20th century's form-follows-function lineage
worn as a look: Bauhaus/constructivist geometry (primaries, circles,
diagonals, photomontage) maturing into Swiss/International style (the
grid, Helvetica, asymmetric precision). [[grid-systems]] carries the
skeleton; this entry is the full costume.

## You know it when you see

- **Bauhaus/constructivist register**: circles, triangles, bars in red/yellow/blue + black on cream; diagonal compositions with real energy; type set on angles; photomontage
- **Swiss register**: ruthless grid alignment, one sans family (Helvetica/Univers class), enormous scale contrast, asymmetric layouts balanced by whitespace, flag-red accents
- Both: zero decoration — the geometry and the type *are* the design

## Dials

- **register choice** — Bauhaus (playful-radical: primitives as actors, diagonals, collision energy — [[brutalism]]'s respectable grandparent) vs Swiss (cool-precise: grid obedience, objective photography, hierarchy by scale alone); pick one — they share ancestry but not temperature
- **geometric vocabulary** (Bauhaus) — circle, triangle, square as compositional characters; overlapping with multiply-style color mixing; bars and rules as structure ([[visual-composition]] weight balanced across primitives)
- **the diagonal** (constructivist wing) — one committed angle (15-45°) organizing the composition; type follows it ([[type-composition]] rotation dial, exercised once)
- **palette laws** — Bauhaus: primaries + black on warm paper ([[color-roles]] at maximum constraint); Swiss: black + white + one red (or one accent), photography carrying any other color
- **type discipline** — one grotesk family, tight range of weights, flush-left rag-right ([[text-block-grammar]] law), scale doing all hierarchy work ([[visual-hierarchy]] committed ratios, [[typographic-voice]] at maximum restraint); lowercase-only as a Bauhaus statement
- **whitespace as material** (Swiss) — emptiness is load-bearing ([[spacing-density]] airy register with [[visual-composition]] asymmetric balance); the layout breathes or it isn't Swiss

## Techniques

- **CSS/SVG primitives** — the whole Bauhaus vocabulary is native:
  circles, clip-path triangles, transform rotations; overlap blending
  via `mix-blend-mode: multiply`.
- **Swiss deployment** — [[grid-systems]]' named-line grid + one
  variable grotesk + a red accent token; the craft is deletion
  (run the [[visual-hierarchy]] squint test and remove everything that
  didn't earn its place).
- **Photomontage** — duotone/high-contrast photography ([[duotone-gradient-map]])
  clipped into geometry; the constructivist collage register
  ([[collage-scatter]] with modernist discipline).
- **Modern translation** — this wardrobe *is* contemporary "clean
  design"'s ancestor; going full-costume means exaggerating scale
  contrast and geometry past today's defaults, or it reads as generic
  rather than modernist.
