# Kidcore

A nostalgia register built from late-80s to 2000s childhood:
primary-colour chaos, crayon and marker strokes, stickers, toy plastics,
rainbows, clip art and cartoon energy. Where [[cute-naive]]'s kawaii is
engineered and soft, kidcore is loud, saturated and gleefully cluttered,
and it's ironic and sincere at once.

## You know it when you see

- Red, yellow, blue, green at full saturation, plus rainbow gradients and
  neon
- Crayon, marker and glitter-gel-pen textures; wobbly hand-drawn lines
- Stickers, smiley faces, stars, hearts and toy shapes scattered
  everywhere
- Chunky rounded type, bubble letters, early clip-art and WordArt energy
- Plastic toy surfaces: glossy primaries, building bricks, beads
- The failure: unreadable chaos. Kidcore still needs a calm reading path
  for the content that matters

## Dials

- **saturation**: full primaries (classic) vs pastel-rainbow (softer,
  closer to kawaii)
- **mess budget**: how much of the page is scatter (stickers, doodles,
  shapes) vs clean content area. Keep text blocks and controls in calm
  zones ([[visual-hierarchy]])
- **texture**: crayon/marker strokes ([[hatching-sketch]]), glitter, plastic
  gloss ([[inflatable]], [[claymorphism]]), paper ([[paper-ephemera]])
- **type**: rounded chunky display, bubble letters ([[inflatable]]), a
  readable rounded sans for body
- **irony vs sincerity**: winking retro (clip art, WordArt) vs earnest
  playfulness; pick one and stay consistent
- **motion**: bouncy, squashy, sticker pop-ins ([[squash-and-stretch]]),
  but respect reduced motion

## Techniques

- **Scatter layer**: SVG stickers and doodles absolutely positioned
  around a clean content column ([[collage-scatter]]); seed their
  positions so the layout is stable.
- **Crayon strokes**: SVG `feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` on strokes,
  or textured brush images; one wobble style throughout.
- **Palette tokens**: map the primaries to roles (action, highlight,
  categories) so the chaos still carries meaning.
- **Plastic gloss**: highlight gradients and inner shadows on chunky
  shapes ([[claymorphism]] recipe, more saturated).
