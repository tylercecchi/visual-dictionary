# Memphis Style

The anti-rules wardrobe — 1981 Milan's postmodern rebellion worn as a
look: clashing pastels and brights, squiggles and confetti geometry,
patterns colliding on purpose, furniture-as-toy energy. The style that
made "wrong" combinations a discipline — and the ancestor of every
"playful startup" illustration system.

## You know it when you see

- Squiggles, zigzags, half-circles, and lollipop shapes scattered like confetti
- Black-and-white pattern passages (grids, dots, the "bacterio" squiggle texture) colliding with flat pastels and brights
- Shapes on sticks, impossible-toy constructions, deliberate imbalance that stays cheerful
- The Saved-by-the-Bell / 80s-90s corporate-fun register

## Dials

- **shape confetti** — the vocabulary: squiggle, zigzag, half-disc, triangle, wavy rule, lollipop dot; scattered with seeded jitter ([[collage-scatter]]'s cluster law keeps it composed rather than random — Memphis *looks* chaotic and is balanced, [[visual-composition]] doing secret work)
- **pattern collision** — 2-3 patterns per composition (b/w grid, dot field, squiggle texture) meeting at hard edges with no transition; the collision is the statement ([[border-stroke]] hard boundaries, zero blending)
- **palette** — pastels (mint, pink, lavender) + a few full brights + black-and-white pattern passages; grounds stay light; [[color-roles]] holds loosely — the accent budget is knowingly overspent, but value contrast keeps it legible
- **the terrazzo register** — chip-scatter fields ([[stone-veining]]'s terrazzo, Memphis's surface of choice) as grounds and card textures
- **dimensional wing** — shapes with hard [[long-shadow-isometric]] offsets or simple extrusions; drop shadows as flat offset duplicates (the pre-blur shadow — [[brutalism]]'s neo-shadow is this shadow's grandchild)
- **restraint dial (modern deployment)** — full Memphis is a poster/brand-moment costume; product UI borrows *one element* (a squiggle divider, terrazzo empty-state ground, confetti [[particles]] burst) over a calm system — the full costume everywhere reads as a children's brand

## Techniques

- **SVG shape kit** — the confetti vocabulary as a symbol library,
  placed with seeded transforms ([[generative-art]] seed law);
  squiggles as sine-ish paths with round caps.
- **CSS patterns** — grids/dots via `repeating-linear-gradient` /
  `radial-gradient` tiles; pattern-collision via hard-edged clip-path
  panels.
- **Terrazzo grounds** — [[stone-veining]]'s chip-scatter technique in
  Memphis palettes.
- **Motion** — shapes drift/rotate slowly ([[motion-ceremony]] ambient
  whisper) or burst as [[particles]] confetti on celebration beats.
