# Fashion Editorial

The glossy wardrobe — high-fashion magazine language: enormous elegant
serifs sharing space with photography, sparse pages that spend whole
spreads on one image, type overlapping the model, white space as luxury.
The register where restraint and drama coexist.

## You know it when you see

- A high-contrast didone serif (Vogue's voice) at massive scale, often overlapping or tucked behind the photo's subject
- One photograph given an entire viewport; credits in tiny tracked caps
- Sparse pages: a headline, ten words of standfirst, air
- Black-and-white photography with one accent; hairline rules as the only furniture

## Dials

- **the didone voice** — high-contrast serifs (thin hairlines, dramatic thicks) at display scale; tight leading, elegant tracking; the typeface *is* half the register ([[typographic-voice]] at its most identifying — Didot/Bodoni class, verify licensing)
- **type-photo interleave** — the signature move: headline layers *behind* the subject ([[knockout-typography]]'s z-surgery), or rides moody negative space in the photograph ([[image-treatment]] art direction: shoot/crop *for* the type); type and image as one composition, not caption-and-picture
- **luxury spending** — whole viewports on single images, tiny content per screen ([[spacing-density]] at maximum air — the inverse of [[newspaper-editorial]]: emptiness *is* the money); [[visual-composition]] carries pages that have almost nothing on them
- **credit grammar** — the fashion-credit block: tiny (9-10px equivalent), wide-tracked caps, designer/photographer credits as whispered footnotes ([[text-block-grammar]] label register at minimum volume)
- **photographic register** — editorial B/W or desaturated grades ([[duotone-gradient-map]]), studio [[scene-staging]] or location moodiness ([[gobo-shadows]], [[rim-light]]); imagery quality is a *content contract* — the register collapses on weak photography faster than any other
- **accent discipline** — monochrome + at most one accent (a red, a gold); [[color-roles]] at its most austere; season/issue may rotate the accent (the [[seasonal-theming]] of glossies)

## Techniques

- **Interleave** — the duplicated-masked-image layer technique
  ([[knockout-typography]]): photo → type → photo-subject-cutout on
  top; masks authored or segmented.
- **Fluid didone display** — `clamp()` scale with tightened
  `letter-spacing` at size ([[typographic-voice]] tracking law);
  italic swashes for single accent words.
- **Spread rhythm** — [[centered-stack]] sections alternating
  full-bleed image viewports with near-empty type pages
  ([[scroll-choreography]] at whisper: slow fades only).
- **Deployment** — luxury/beauty/portfolio products, campaign heroes;
  the credit grammar and didone voice transfer to any premium register
  without the full sparseness.
