# Cute / Naive

The deliberate-innocence wardrobe — styles that weaponize childlike
charm: kawaii's engineered cuteness, kidcore's crayon chaos, naive art's
sincere wobble, kitsch's knowing tackiness, rubberhose's 1920s cartoon
bounce. All trade polish for warmth — each by different, *disciplined*
means.

## You know it when you see

- **Kawaii**: round everything, dot-eyes-and-blush faces on objects, pastel + one pop color
- **Kidcore**: primary-color chaos, crayon strokes, stickers, rainbow excess
- **Naive**: wobbly sincere lines, wrong-on-purpose proportions, visible hand
- **Kitsch**: gloriously too-much — airbrushed sunsets, gold frames, winking tackiness
- **Rubberhose**: noodle limbs, pie-cut eyes, bouncing everything (the Cuphead register)

## Dials

- **register pick** — kawaii (engineered: strict simplification rules), kidcore ([[collage-scatter]] + [[stickers-badges]] + crayon [[hatching-sketch]] texture), naive ([[illustration-style]] hand register with wobble kept), kitsch ([[period-looks]] logic: sincerity-vs-wink dial mandatory), rubberhose (animation-first: [[motion-ceremony]] squash-and-stretch at maximum)
- **kawaii construction (the engineered one)** — faces via minimum features (dot eyes wide apart + tiny mouth + optional blush ovals) applied to *anything* (the anthropomorphism move — food, objects, [[chips-badges-avatars]] mascot avatars); proportions squashed (2-3 head-heights); [[shape-language]] radius maxed, no sharp corners anywhere; palette pastel + one saturated accent
- **the wobble discipline** (naive/kidcore) — imperfection is *consistent*: one line weight that wobbles the same way everywhere ([[hatching-sketch]]'s seeded-wobble law), crayon/marker texture on all strokes or none; mixed polish levels read as error, uniform naivety reads as voice
- **cuteness physics** — motion bounces ([[interaction-feel]] low damping, [[motion-ceremony]] overshoot at maximum license), sounds would squeak, everything is [[inflatable]]-adjacent soft; hover = delight beats ([[hover-response]] with personality: wiggle, blink)
- **mascot integration** — the register's ambassador ([[illustration-style]] mascot dial): reacts to states (error = sad face — [[form-grammar]] feedback with charm), celebrates ([[particles]] confetti), guides ([[coach-marks]] presenter)
- **dose & audience** — full costume for kids' products, games, sticker/merch brands; general products borrow *one inhabitant* (a mascot, kawaii empty states) over calm systems; kitsch specifically requires the wink to land or it's just tacky ([[visible-accessibility]] note: cute never excuses low contrast)

## Techniques

- **Kawaii faces** — a parametric SVG face kit (eye spacing, blush,
  mouth set) stamped onto shapes; the simplification rules make this
  genuinely componentizable.
- **Crayon/wobble strokes** — turbulence-displaced paths + rough
  texture fills ([[chalk-blackboard]] machinery in wax register);
  seeded so it's stable.
- **Rubberhose motion** — extreme squash-stretch keyframes, curved
  limb paths (bezier "hoses"), everything on bounce easing; Lottie or
  spring-driven SVG.
- **Sticker assembly** — [[stickers-badges]] die-cut borders + drop
  shadows + [[collage-scatter]] placement for kidcore compositions.
