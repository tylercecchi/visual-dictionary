# Art Nouveau

The organic-line wardrobe — 1890s-1900s nature worn as ornament: whiplash
curves, vine borders, women with flowing hair in halo frames (the Mucha
poster), botanical everything. Where [[art-deco]] is ruler-built, Nouveau
is *grown* — every line a plant's gesture.

## You know it when you see

- Long S-curves that snap back on themselves (the "whiplash"); no straight lines anywhere important
- Content framed by arches, halos, and circular cartouches wrapped in vines
- Muted natural palettes — sage, ochre, dusty rose, cream — with flat poster color inside strong outlines
- Lettering that bends and flows as if it were another vine

## Dials

- **the whiplash line** — asymmetric S-curves with accelerating curvature (a bezier whose handles are deliberately unequal); line weight *swells and tapers* along the stroke (the calligraphic profile — [[hatching-sketch]]'s pressure logic applied to ornament); uniform-width curves read as clip-art
- **frame grammar** — the halo/arch cartouche around the subject (the Mucha structure: circle behind the head, arch over the figure, rectangular border with corner blooms); content *inhabits* ornament rather than sitting beside it ([[magazine-spread]] overlap courage, 1900 edition)
- **botanical vocabulary** — stylized (not naturalistic) irises, lilies, poppies, peacock feathers, dragonflies; each simplified to flat shapes + outline ([[illustration-style]] flat-with-line register)
- **palette** — low-chroma naturals ([[color-roles]]: desaturated secondaries, warm cream grounds — never white), flat fills inside dark-olive or brown outlines (not black); gold accents sparing
- **type register** — flowing, hand-drawn display with organic terminals; letters may merge with the ornament ([[knockout-typography]] text-on-curve, [[typographic-voice]] at its most illustrative)
- **the Tiffany register** — the dimensional wing: [[stained-glass]] with nouveau linework as the leading; lamp/window compositions

## Techniques

- **SVG calligraphic curves** — paths with `stroke-width` varied via
  multiple offset paths or converted to filled tapering outlines
  (vector tools do this natively; programmatically: perpendicular
  offset by a width profile); the swell-taper is the craft.
- **Frame kits** — reusable cartouche components (halo + arch + border)
  with content slots; corner blooms as symbols.
- **Poster pipeline** — flat fills + outline over a cream paper ground
  ([[material-texture]]), slight [[print-artifacts]] wear for the
  century-old-lithograph read.
- **Modern deployment** — nouveau borders on [[focus-page]] cards and
  hero frames; the full figure-poster register for editorial/brand
  moments only.
