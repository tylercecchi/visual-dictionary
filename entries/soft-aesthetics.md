# Soft Aesthetics

The gentleness wardrobe — the family of registers whose shared promise
is *softness*: cottagecore's rural coziness, boho's layered warmth,
shabby chic's faded romance, coquette's delicate sweetness, Japandi's
calm restraint, ethereal's dreamlike air. Different costumes, one
physics: low contrast, warm or airy light, organic texture, nothing
hard-edged.

## You know it when you see

- Cream/linen grounds, desaturated warm palettes, serif or hand voices
- Organic textures everywhere — paper, linen, dried flowers, watercolor washes
- Soft light: hazy, backlit, golden ([[time-of-day]] perpetual golden hour)
- The failures: softness without structure (mush), or a hard modern component dropped in unadapted (a neon CTA in a cottage)

## Dials

- **register pick** — cottagecore (rural: gingham, botanical [[illustration-style]], kraft [[material-texture]]), boho (layered: terracotta-sage-mustard, woven textures, [[ornament-traditions]] folk patterns at low contrast), shabby chic (faded romance: [[weathering-patina]] paint-wear + pastel florals + [[print-artifacts]] age), coquette (delicate: bows, lace [[ornament-traditions]] filigree, blush pastels, fine serifs), Japandi (the restrained wing: [[spacing-density]] airy + neutral naturals + wabi-sabi imperfection — softness via *absence*), ethereal ([[progressive-blur]] + [[mesh-gradient]] pale fields + [[glow-bloom]] whisper — softness via *atmosphere*)
- **the softness physics (shared law)** — contrast ceiling lowered globally ([[color-roles]]: foregrounds warm-dark, never black; whites are cream), [[shape-language]] generous radii or organic edges, [[elevation]] via soft wide shadows or none, [[light-on-flat]] warm and diffuse
- **texture obligation** — flat digital color breaks every register: grounds carry [[material-texture]] (linen, paper, plaster) or [[film-grain]] at minimum; botanical/floral elements as [[illustration-style]] line-or-watercolor, never stock-photo hard
- **type voice** — high-contrast serifs or gentle hands ([[typographic-voice]] warm registers); Japandi alone goes light grotesk; display flourishes ([[art-nouveau]] echoes) for coquette/shabby
- **motion register** — slow, eased, minimal ([[motion-ceremony]] calm end): drifts, gentle fades, [[kinetic-type]] at its quietest; springs damped ([[interaction-feel]] heavy-soft)
- **structure insurance** — softness needs secret rigor: [[grid-systems]] and [[visual-hierarchy]] fully active under the texture, or the register collapses into mush — the same secret-balance law as [[memphis-style]], opposite temperament

## Techniques

- **Ground recipes** — cream + linen texture + slight [[vignette]]
  warmth; ethereal: [[mesh-gradient]] pales + [[progressive-blur]]
  edges.
- **Botanical kits** — line-drawn or watercolor-wash florals (roadmap:
  watercolor entry) as SVG/raster accents; corner and divider
  placements per [[visual-composition]].
- **Fade pass** (shabby/coquette) — desaturate + lift blacks
  ([[duotone-gradient-map]] gentle ramp) + [[weathering-patina]]
  worn-edge on panels.
- **Component adaptation** — every [[control-language]] control
  re-skinned to the softness physics (cream fills, warm-dark text,
  radius up, focus rings warm) — the register lives or dies in the
  controls.
