# Historical Ornate

The pre-modern grandeur wardrobe — the great ornate eras as costumes:
Gothic's dark verticality, Baroque's swirling drama, Rococo's pastel
froth, Victorian's dense eclecticism, Neoclassicism's austere symmetry.
Requested constantly as "gothic," "vintage label," "dark academia,"
"romantasy" — each resolves to an era recipe here.

## You know it when you see

- **Gothic**: blackletter type, pointed-arch verticality, black/crimson/gold, heraldry
- **Baroque**: swirling acanthus scrollwork, deep shadow drama, gold on dark
- **Rococo**: the pastel, asymmetric, shell-and-froth lightening of Baroque
- **Victorian**: dense layered ornament, etched illustration, elaborate label lockups (the whiskey-label register)
- **Neoclassical**: Greek/Roman restraint — columns, laurels, symmetry, cream and stone

## Dials

- **era pick** — each fixes ornament density, palette temperature, and type; "dark academia" ≈ Victorian-neoclassical blend; "romantasy" ≈ Gothic + romantic florals + [[firelight-flicker]] mood
- **gothic recipe** — blackletter display ([[typographic-voice]] at maximum costume; body text never — legibility law), pointed-arch frames, [[stained-glass]], heraldic devices ([[ornament-traditions]] adjacency), [[time-of-day]] night + candlelit [[firelight-flicker]]; palette: near-black, crimson, antique gold
- **baroque recipe** — acanthus scroll cartouches (swirling filled ornament — the carved register of [[art-nouveau]]'s line), **tenebrism lighting** (extreme dark-light drama — [[exposure-adaptation]] low-key + [[gobo-shadows]] single-source), gold [[specular-metallic]] on deep grounds, [[velvet-plush]] and [[stone-veining]] materials
- **victorian recipe** — the ornate *label* lockup: layered banners, rules, and flourishes around center-axis type ([[type-composition]] centered totem + [[border-stroke]] multi-rule frames), etched illustration ([[hatching-sketch]]/[[woodcut-linocut]] fine register), [[print-artifacts]] aged paper; density is the point — Victorian whitespace reads as unfinished
- **neoclassical recipe** — the restraint wing: symmetric [[visual-composition]], laurel/column/urn motifs as thin [[border-stroke]] linework, stone palette ([[concrete-plaster]] warmth), wide-tracked caps ([[typographic-voice]] lapidary register)
- **deployment dose** — full costume for brand-lore, packaging-style heroes, games; product UI borrows the *lockup logic* (Victorian center-axis title blocks) or one frame, over modern systems

## Techniques

- **Ornament kits** — era-correct SVG components (cartouches, banners,
  corners, laurels); Baroque scrollwork is drawn/sourced (it resists
  proceduralization — unlike [[art-deco]]); Victorian lockups compose
  from banner + rule + flourish primitives on a center axis.
- **Blackletter** — display-only faces, generous size, never tight
  tracking; pair with a humanist body face.
- **Aging pass** — [[print-artifacts]] + [[material-texture]] paper +
  [[weathering-patina]] gold-tarnish, dosed per era.
- **Tenebrism** — one hard key ([[gobo-shadows]]) + crushed low-key
  grade ([[exposure-adaptation]]) + warm highlights; the Baroque
  lighting in three moves.
