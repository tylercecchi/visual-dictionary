# Instructional Manual

The how-to wardrobe — instruction design's registers: IKEA's wordless
line-art, airline safety-card pictograms, numbered step-by-step guides,
quick-starts. The discipline of explaining a *procedure* visually, where
clarity is measured by whether the shelf gets built.

## You know it when you see

- Numbered steps in boxes, one action each, in unmistakable order
- Line-art diagrams: the object outlined, the *action* emphasized (arrows, motion dashes, the moving part bold)
- Wordless registers: pictograms + arrows + check/cross marks doing all the talking
- Callout details: a circled zoom of the tricky part; part inventories ("2× screw A") up front
- The failures: steps combining three actions, arrows ambiguous about direction, the critical warning buried

## Dials

- **one-action law** — each step is exactly one operation; compound steps are where users fail; step count honesty over step count vanity ([[navigation-language]] stepper truth in print form)
- **register** — wordless (IKEA: universal, high drawing burden — every action must be *drawable*), captioned (numbered figure + one imperative sentence — the default), photo-based (screenshots/photos with [[coach-marks]]-style overlays — software's register), safety-card (pictogram + color severity)
- **diagram grammar** — [[blueprint]]'s linework rules softened: object in uniform light outline, the *acting part* emphasized (weight, fill, or accent [[color-roles]]), action arrows (bold, curved for rotation, doubled for repetition — the arrow vocabulary is the language), motion dashes, before/after ghosts ([[cutaway-xray]] ghosting for placement)
- **detail callouts** — circled magnifications ([[cutaway-xray]] lens register) tethered to their location; part inventory as a [[table-design]]-lite manifest with counts ("4× M6") and true-scale silhouettes
- **warning hierarchy** — caution/warning/danger as a strict [[color-roles]] semantic ladder with pictograms ([[chips-badges-avatars]] severity logic), positioned *before* the step they guard, never after
- **progress & wayfinding** — step numbers big and scannable ([[animated-numbers]]' tabular stability in static form), progress indication on digital ([[navigation-language]] stepper), tools-needed preamble; the quick-start vs full-manual split ([[disclosure]]: the 90% path up front, depth behind)

## Techniques

- **Step components** — numbered figure boxes on a consistent grid
  ([[grid-systems]]); digital: one step per screen/scroll beat
  ([[scroll-choreography]] or stepper-paged).
- **Diagram kit** — SVG line-art at 2 weights (object hairline, action
  bold) + the arrow library (straight/curved/rotate/repeat) as
  symbols; consistency of the arrow vocabulary *is* the clarity.
- **Software register** — screenshots with [[coach-marks]] spotlight
  ellipses/arrows burned in; keep annotation style identical across
  every figure.
- **Animated steps** — the premium digital move: each diagram a
  2-3 second loop of its action ([[motion-ceremony]] utility timing,
  pausable — [[visible-accessibility]]); [[layout-motion]] ghosts for
  part placement.
